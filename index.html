Research

Supply Networks and Market Power in U.S. Poultry Procurement

Job Market Paper

Using restricted Census shipment microdata, I construct the first shipment-based U.S. poultry procurement network, identifying the plants and firms competing for local purchases, and combine it with a demand-and-supply model of heterogeneous procurement decisions and joint pricing by multi-plant firms. Wholesalers are 3.33 times as price sensitive as food-service buyers, counties with a larger wholesale-channel share procure more and lose more under input-cost shocks, and a merger of the third- and fourth-largest processors raises prices by 6.9 percent.
